WHY IT MATTERS

The first step to prevention is understanding.

Cardiovascular diseases (CVDs) are the leading cause of death worldwide. The first symptom can be a heart attack, caused by a blockage preventing blood from flowing to different areas in your body.

Many cardiovascular diseases can present in a patient due to behavioural and environmental risk factors that often go unnoticed and ignored.
