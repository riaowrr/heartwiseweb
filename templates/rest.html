STEP 3 | REST & RESET

Give your heart a break.

Sleep and relaxing yourself matters more than one would think.

The main idea: If your body is in constant fight-or-flight mode, so is your heart. Find the balance and respect it.

Habits to build

Teenagers require 8-10 hours of sleep, while adults need 7-9 hours.

Sleep at the same time every night and wake up at the same time. Doing this will fix your natural circadian rhythm.

Get outside every morning so that your body can power through the day and you won't have to solely rely on caffeine.

Limit your screen time a few hours before bed to avoid disrupting your natural circadian rhythm and stress your heart.

Regulate your emotions when things get heated with breathing exercises and meditation to avoid chronic stress.

Acknowledge your blessings in life and take a few minutes a day to be appreciative.

Why does this matter?

Giving your body the rest it needs helps you avoid a high blood pressure, obesity, chronic stress, chronic anger, worry, and hostility.
