STEP 1 | EAT

Feed your heart.

Eating to improve your heart health means consciously making the right choices to fuel your body.

The main idea: Mindful eating isn't about entering yourself into a restrictive diet, but it's about eating healthy MOST of the time. Never look down on yourself for indulging in a craving and protect your mental health first.

Habits to build

Eat fiber first, then follow it up with protein and healthy fats, and finally end with carbs. Following an order to meals reduces your blood sugar from spiking and helps your body process glucose more efficiently and safely.

Found in berries, dark chocolate, and green and black tea. They reduce inflammation and lower blood pressure.

Favor plant-based proteins as they are the safest option for your whole-body health. It includes legumes, nuts, and seeds. However, lean poultry and eggs should still be consumed moderately over the week. For example, eggs contain important carotenoids and have amino acid arginine. In addition, red meat should be apart of your diet, but should be limited to 1-3 times a week.

Known to increase 'good' cholesterol instead of 'bad' cholesterol, unlike saturated fats. Avocadoes, olives, nuts, and seeds, are crucial to your heart health.

1. Sweet potatoes have more potassium than bananas! 2. Papayas are also important to note as they are rich in lycopene. 3. Yogurt undergoes a bacterial fermentation process that creates specific peptides that have an impact on bettering blood pressure. 4. Peanuts are found to have amino acid l-arginine and vitamin E.

Why does this matter?

Junk and highly processed foods heighten 'bad' cholesterol, leading to plaque build-up in your arteries and block blood flow.
