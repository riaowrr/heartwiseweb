STEP 2 | MOVE

Exercise your heart.

Make your heart pump faster on purpose.

The main idea: Physical activity can be as simple as walking around the house. Do not overcommit to a strict regime and randomly stop. Take your time.

Habits to build

Walking at a brisk speed for 3 minutes, then slowing it down for 3 minutes, and continuing this cycle for up to 30 minutes a day will elevate your heart rate and increase blood flow to your muscles. The consistent switching from zone 3 to zone 1 cardio directly improves heart health.

The viral goal of achieving 10k steps a day was found to have originated from a Japanese marketing campaign for a pedometer app. Studies show that the benefits peak at 7,200 steps and then plateau.

Walking (even just around the house) for 2-5 minutes after each meal stabilises insulin levels and keeps your blood sugar from its spike 60-90 minutes after finishing a meal.

Training with weights and/or progressively overloading in the gym 2 times a week reduces fat and keeps you from being unhealthily overweight.

Using the stairs instead of a lift daily can reduce your risk of CVDs. Specifically, aiming for at least 50 stair steps a day is shown to decrease this risk by 20%.

Why does this matter?

Physical activity helps you maintain a healthy weight and avoid obesity. It strengthens your heart muscle and therefore improves your heart's ability to pump blood throughout your body. Moreover, the oxygen levels in your blood rise as capillaries widen.
